# StockỌja app guide: pages, links and how to do things

This guide explains how to find your way around the StockỌja app: every page in the menu, its web address, what it
shows, and step-by-step procedures for common tasks. The app is at https://app.stockoja.com and works in the
browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Page names below are exactly as they appear in the app's menu.

Staff only see the pages their role allows. If a page described here is missing from your menu, your role does
not include it; ask the business owner.

## Getting into the app

### Important links

- Sign up (free): https://app.stockoja.com/register
- Log in: https://app.stockoja.com/login
- Forgot password: https://app.stockoja.com/forgot-password
- Dashboard after logging in: https://app.stockoja.com/dashboard
- Website: https://www.stockoja.com
- Pricing: https://www.stockoja.com/pricing
- Help and FAQ: https://www.stockoja.com/help
- Contact: WhatsApp +234 911 346 2780 or support@stockoja.com

### Signing up

1. Open https://app.stockoja.com/register.
2. Enter your first name, last name, email, a password and your business name. No card is needed.
3. You go straight into setup. A confirmation email is sent; you can confirm it later.

You can also sign up and sign in with a Google account.

### The setup steps after sign-up

Setup is at https://app.stockoja.com/onboarding. It saves your progress, so you can stop and come back on any device.

1. Store name and web address: choose yourname.stockoja.com. It is checked as you type and cannot be changed later.
2. Store look: pick a design, add your logo and the WhatsApp number customers can message.
3. Delivery: pickup only, delivery areas with a fee for each, or "I'll arrange delivery with customers myself".
4. Bank account for payouts (can be skipped): where online sales are paid.
5. First product (can be skipped): name, price, quantity and an optional photo.
6. Finish: publish your store if you added a bank account, or go to the dashboard.

### Logging in and forgotten passwords

Log in at https://app.stockoja.com/login with your email and password, or with Google. If you turned on two-step
verification, enter the code from your authenticator app or email. If you forgot your password, use
https://app.stockoja.com/forgot-password and follow the reset link sent to your email.

### Joining a business as staff

When an owner or manager invites you, you get an email. Open the link in it to set up your account and join the
business. You then see only the pages and the store your role allows.

## Finding your way around

### The menu

The menu is on the left on a computer, and behind the menu button on a phone. It has two groups.

Day-to-day pages: Dashboard, POS, Products, Inventory, Orders, Customers, Suppliers & purchases, Expenses,
Promotions, Reports.

Business pages: Online store, Payouts, Stores, Staff, Roles & permissions, Activity log, Plan & billing, Settings,
Help & support.

### Choosing a store (branch)

At the top of the app, the active store shows which branch you are working in. Owners and business-wide managers
can switch store, or view all stores together where a page allows it. Store-level staff only ever see their own
store.

### Help inside the app

Press the ? (Help) button at the top of the app to:

- Take the tour: a one-minute look around the menu.
- Show me how to: step-by-step walkthroughs that guide you through a task on the real page (add a product, record a
  sale, record an expense, review your stock).
- Open the getting-started checklist.
- Contact support.

### The getting-started checklist

On the dashboard, the Getting started checklist lists: add your first product, review your stock, record your first
sale, record an expense, and view or share your online store. Each item ticks itself off when you really do it, and
each has a "Show me" button that starts its walkthrough. You can hide the checklist and bring it back from Help.

## Day-to-day pages

### Dashboard

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/dashboard

The home page. It shows today's sales against yesterday, the last 7 days against the 7 days before, gross profit,
open online orders, a sales chart (net sales per day, with gross profit as the lighter part of each bar), best
sellers, low stock (items at or below their alert level) and anything needing attention, such as orders to confirm.

### POS

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/pos

The till for the counter. Find a product, take payment, and stock goes down by itself. It keeps working when the
internet goes off and syncs later.

### Sales history

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/pos/sales

In-store sales with receipts, returns and voids. Search by receipt number or customer and open any sale to see its
items, payments, returns and activity.

### Products

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/products

Your catalogue: one list for every store. Each store keeps its own stock and can change price and visibility.
Related pages:

- Add product (full form): https://app.stockoja.com/products/new
- Quick add products (several in rows): https://app.stockoja.com/products/quick-add
- Create a product from a photo (AI): https://app.stockoja.com/products/ai
- Import products from CSV: https://app.stockoja.com/products/import
- Categories & brands: https://app.stockoja.com/products/categories

### Inventory

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/inventory

Stock on hand, reserved for online orders, and available to sell in each store. Filter by low stock or out of stock.
Click a product to see its stock details: stock at each branch and every stock movement, with who made it and why.

### Stock transfers

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/inventory/transfers

Send stock between stores. It leaves when sent and is added once the receiving store accepts it.

### Orders

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/orders

Online orders from your store. Confirm them, mark them ready (or out for delivery), and hand them over with the
customer's pickup code.

### Customers

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/customers

Your customers across all stores, with their purchase history: what they bought, how often and how much.

### Suppliers & purchases

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/suppliers

Who you buy from, and the stock you have ordered from them through purchase orders. New purchase order:
https://app.stockoja.com/suppliers/orders/new

### Expenses

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/expenses

Rent, transport, salaries and other running costs, by store and category. Shows the total spent for the period,
the number of entries and the biggest category.

### Promotions

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/promotions

Coupon codes and automatic discounts for the till and your online store. Included on the Thrive and Empire plans.

### Reports

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/reports

Sales, products, inventory and customer reports for a period and a store, with CSV export. Included from the
Scale plan.

## Business pages

### Online store

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/storefront

Brand your store, preview it and publish when you are ready. Its sections are: Address & domain, Theme, Branding,
Home page, Contact, Social links, Policies, Search engines, and Poster & link. Buttons: Preview, Publish (or Publish changes),
and Take offline. When the store is live you can download your shop poster with its QR code.

Your published store is at https://yourname.stockoja.com.

### Payouts

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/payouts

Online payments are paid straight into your bank account by Paystack, after StockỌja's platform fee and Paystack's
fee. Add or change your bank account here, and see the last 30 days of online sales: sales, fees and what was paid
to your bank. Changing an existing bank account needs two-step verification.

### Stores

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/stores

Your branches. Stock, sales, staff and expenses are tracked per store. Add a store, edit its details or deactivate
it. Nothing is deleted when a store is deactivated, and it can be reactivated. Your plan sets how many stores you
can have.

### Staff

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/staff

Invite your team and control what each person can do. Staff are never deleted, only deactivated, so their sales
history stays intact.

### Roles & permissions

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/roles

Built-in roles (owner, manager, cashier) cover most teams. Create custom roles when you need something different,
either for the whole business or for one store.

### Activity log

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/activity

A permanent record of important changes in your business: who did what, and when.

### Plan & billing

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/subscription

Your plan, how much of it you are using (stores, staff, products), what each plan includes, and your billing
history. Use "See plans" or "Compare plans" to upgrade or change plan. Paid plans are paid through Paystack.

### Settings

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/settings

Settings has these sections:

- Account: your name, phone and email.
- Security: password, two-step verification, Google sign-in and the devices you are signed in on.
- Notifications: what you are told about, and where (in the app, email, push).
- Appearance: how StockỌja looks on this device.
- Business profile: your business details and logo, shown on receipts and your online store.
- Payments & tax: how shoppers pay for online orders, VAT on sales (off by default) and your online store address.

### Notifications

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/notifications

Stock alerts, online orders, transfers and team updates for you. You can turn on push notifications to get alerts
on your phone or computer even when StockỌja is not open.

### Help & support

Link: https://app.stockoja.com/support

Ask the StockỌja team for help from inside the app. Press "New request", describe the problem and send. The team
replies there and by email.

## How to: products and stock

### How to add one product

1. Go to Products and press Add product, then choose "Add one product".
2. Type the name the way customers know it, for example "Ankara fabric (6 yards)".
3. Enter the selling price. Add a cost price too if you want to see your profit.
4. Enter how many you have now at each branch. Leave 0 if you are not sure and count later.
5. Add a photo if you like (it helps in your online store), then press Save.

### How to add several products quickly

1. Go to Products, press Add product and choose quick add (or open https://app.stockoja.com/products/quick-add).
2. Type one product per row. Only the name and selling price are needed.
3. Save. You can add photos and more details later.

### How to add a product from a photo with AI

1. Open https://app.stockoja.com/products/ai (Products → Add product → from a photo).
2. Upload one clear photo of the product.
3. AI suggests the name, category, brand, description and any colours or sizes it can see.
4. Check the suggestions, add your price and quantity, and save. AI never sets prices or stock.

### How to import products from a spreadsheet

1. Open https://app.stockoja.com/products/import and press Download template.
2. Fill in one product per row. Only name and selling_price are required; the other columns (SKU, low-stock level,
   opening stock and so on) are optional. Save it as CSV (in Excel: File → Save As → CSV).
3. Choose the store for opening stock from the store menu at the top.
4. Press Choose CSV file, then Check file. Rows with problems are listed so you can fix them.
5. Press Import. Files must be under 900 KB; split bigger ones.

### How to add sizes and colours

Open the product and add variants, for example sizes S, M, L or colours red and black. Each variant has its own
SKU, barcode, price and stock.

### How to correct stock after a count

1. Go to Inventory and open the product.
2. Choose Adjust stock, enter the new quantity or the change, and pick a reason (stock count, damaged, expired,
   lost).
3. Confirm the adjustment. It is recorded in the stock history with your name and cannot be deleted.

### How to move stock to another branch

1. Go to Inventory → Stock transfers and press New transfer.
2. Choose the store to send to, add the products and quantities, and send.
3. At the receiving store, open the transfer, enter what actually arrived (or Accept everything) and Confirm
   receipt. Anything that did not arrive goes back to the sending store.

### How to see what is running low

Go to Inventory and filter by "Low stock" or "Out of stock", or check the Low stock list on the dashboard. Set each
product's low-stock level so you are alerted in time.

## How to: selling

### How to record a sale at the POS

1. Open POS and choose the store you are selling from.
2. Find a product by typing its name or scanning its barcode, and tap it to add it to the cart. Tap again to add
   more.
3. Check the cart: change quantities, remove items, add a discount or a customer if needed.
4. Press Pay and choose how the customer is paying: cash, card, transfer, or split across methods. For cash, the
   change is worked out for you.
5. Confirm the sale. Stock goes down and a receipt is ready to print or share.

### How to return items or void a sale

1. Go to Sales history (https://app.stockoja.com/pos/sales) and open the sale.
2. To take back some items, choose Return items, pick the items and quantities, and confirm. The stock goes back.
3. If the whole sale was a mistake, choose Void sale and give a reason. All items go back into stock. A void cannot
   be undone, and the sale stays on record as voided.

### How to sell when the internet is off

Open the POS online once on each device first, so it has your products and prices. After that, if the connection
drops, keep selling as normal: the header shows that sales will sync later, and they sync automatically when you
are back online. Coupons, held sales, voids and adding new customers need a connection.

### How to handle an online order

1. Go to Orders and open the new order.
2. Press Confirm order.
3. Prepare the items and mark the order ready for pickup, or out for delivery. The customer is emailed at each
   step.
4. For pickup: when the customer arrives, choose Hand over order and check their pickup code. For delivery: mark
   it delivered once it arrives. Stock and sales are then updated.

If you cancel an order that was paid online, the customer is refunded automatically through Paystack.

### How to create a coupon or promotion

On the Thrive or Empire plan, go to Promotions and press New promotion. Give it a name (shown on receipts), a
code such as EASTER10 for a coupon, a percentage or amount off, and optionally a minimum order, start and end
dates, total uses and uses per customer. Choose where it applies (in-store POS, online store, or both) and save.

## How to: the online store

### How to publish your online store

1. Go to Online store. If you skipped setup steps, a launch checklist shows what is left.
2. Choose your store address (once only), pick a theme, and add your logo and colours.
3. Choose pickup, delivery areas with fees, or "I'll arrange delivery myself" (also in Settings → Payments & tax).
4. Add your bank account in Payouts; it is needed before you can publish.
5. Make sure your products are visible online, press Preview to check, then Publish.

After the first publish, download your shop poster with its QR code to print or share on WhatsApp, Instagram or
Facebook.

### How to set up delivery areas and fees

Go to Settings → Payments & tax. Under Delivery, choose delivery areas and fees, press Add area, and enter each
area and what you charge (for example Lekki ₦2,500). Choose the branch deliveries are sent from and save. The same
options appear in the online store launch steps.

### How to change your store's look

Go to Online store, choose a theme, and change your logo, colours, banner and headline. Use Preview, then Publish
changes. Your products, colours, logo and details carry over to any theme. Themes include Prime Avenue (a busy,
marketplace-style shop for large catalogues), Velvet Atelier (calm and editorial, for fashion, beauty and
lifestyle), Blossom Aura (soft and luxurious, for perfume and skincare) and Golden Basket (fast and practical, for
supermarkets and groceries).

### How to share your store link

Your store link is https://yourname.stockoja.com. Share it, or a product's link, on WhatsApp, Facebook or X: the
preview shows your banner, logo and name, or the product's photo. You can also share the QR poster on WhatsApp
status.

### How to add or change your payout bank account

Go to Payouts and press Add bank account. Your first account needs no extra step. To change an existing account,
turn on two-step verification first (Settings → Security).

## How to: team, branches and account

### How to add a branch

Go to Stores and press Add store. Enter its name and address and save. Your plan sets how many stores you can add;
upgrade in Plan & billing if you need more.

### How to invite a staff member

1. Go to Staff and press Invite staff.
2. Enter their email, choose their role, and choose their store if the role is for one branch only.
3. Send. They get an email to join your business.

To stop someone using the app, deactivate them. Their history stays.

### How to create a custom role

Go to Roles & permissions and press Create role. Name it, choose whether it is for the whole business or one store,
tick the permissions it needs (for example selling, discounts, returns, stock adjustments, expenses, reports) and
save.

### How to record an expense

1. Go to Expenses and press Add expense.
2. Enter the amount (for example 5000) and a short note you will recognise, such as "Generator fuel".
3. Pick a category (or add your own) and the store, and save.

### How to add a supplier and a purchase order

1. Go to Suppliers & purchases and press Add supplier.
2. Press New purchase order, choose the supplier and the store receiving the goods, and add products with what each
   unit costs you.
3. Mark it ordered. When goods arrive, choose Receive stock and enter what came in. Part deliveries are fine.

### How to see reports and export CSV

On Scale or above, go to Reports, pick a period and a store, and view sales, products, inventory or customers. Use
export to download CSV for your accountant.

### How to upgrade or change plan

Go to Plan & billing and press See plans or Compare plans. Choose a plan and pay through Paystack. You can change
plans any time.

### How to turn on two-step verification

Go to Settings → Security and turn on two-step verification. Use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator,
Microsoft Authenticator, Authy) or codes by email, and keep the backup codes somewhere safe; each works once.

### How to get help

Press ? at the top of the app for the tour and walkthroughs, open Help & support to send a request to the team, or
message +234 911 346 2780 on WhatsApp or email support@stockoja.com.
