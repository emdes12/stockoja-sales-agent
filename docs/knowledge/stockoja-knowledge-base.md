# StockỌja knowledge base

This document describes StockỌja: what it is, who it is for, what it does today, what it costs and how to get
help. Everything here is something the product does now. Where StockỌja does not do something yet, that is said
plainly. Prices are in Nigerian naira and were taken from stockoja.com on 27 October 2026.

## About StockỌja

### What StockỌja is

StockỌja is business management software for shops and growing businesses that sell physical products. It
brings inventory management, a point of sale (POS), sales and expense tracking, customers, suppliers and purchase
orders, staff and branches, and an online store into one connected system. A business has one record of what it
has, what it sold, what it spent and where it is going.

StockỌja runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install.

### The name

Ọjà is the Yoruba word for market. StockỌja means stock and market together: the stock you manage and the market
you sell into. Where the letter Ọ cannot be typed, the name is written StockOja.

### Who StockỌja is for

Businesses that sell physical products and have outgrown notebooks, spreadsheets, WhatsApp chats and memory, from
one shop to several branches. It is built first for businesses in Nigeria: naira pricing, cash, transfer and card
payments, Paystack, and selling that often starts on WhatsApp.

Typical businesses:

- Fashion brands and boutiques
- Phone and accessories shops
- Electronics shops
- Beauty and cosmetics sellers
- Wig and hair businesses
- Food and beverage sellers
- General merchants, supermarkets and other retailers
- Small businesses of any kind that sell products

### Why StockỌja was built

Many good businesses run on notebooks, spreadsheets, WhatsApp chats and memory. That works until the business
grows: a second branch, a few staff, customers ordering online. Then stock goes missing, sales are hard to add up
and nobody is sure what the business actually made.

The tools available were usually one piece of the puzzle (a till, a stock sheet, a website) that did not talk to
each other, or software priced and designed for much larger companies. StockỌja is one connected system that a
shop owner can set up in an afternoon and run from a phone.

### How StockỌja works with your business

- Honest numbers. Stock and money are never quietly changed. Corrections are recorded with a reason, sales are
  voided rather than deleted, and sensitive changes are logged with who made them.
- Your data stays yours. Each business's data is kept separate from every other business. Paid plans can export
  reports as CSV.
- Built for the counter. Fast enough for a queue, simple enough for a new cashier, and usable on a phone.
- No made-up claims. StockỌja would rather tell you what it does not do yet than oversell it.

## Getting started

### Signing up

Sign up at app.stockoja.com/register with your first name, last name, email, a password and your business name.
No card is needed. Every new business starts on the free Hustle plan. A confirmation email is sent, but you can
carry on with setup straight away.

### The setup steps

Right after sign-up, a short setup guides you through:

1. Store name and web address: the link customers use, yourname.stockoja.com. StockỌja checks it is free as you
   type. It is set once and cannot be changed later.
2. Store look: choose a design, add your logo and the WhatsApp number shoppers see.
3. Delivery: pickup only, delivery to areas you set with a fee for each, or a note that you arrange delivery
   with customers yourself.
4. Bank account for payouts: where online sales are paid. You can skip this and add it later, but it is needed
   before the online store can be published.
5. First product: name, price, quantity and an optional photo. You can skip this too.
6. Finish: publish your store, or go to the dashboard.

Setup remembers where you stopped, on any device. Most businesses can sell the same day.

### Help inside the app

After setup, a short tour shows you around. A getting-started checklist on the dashboard ticks itself off as you
really do each task: add a product, make a sale, record an expense, publish your store. Each item has a "Show me"
walkthrough that guides you through it on the real page. The ? button in the header brings any of them back.
Customers can also open Help & support inside the app.

## Products and inventory

### Adding products

There are four ways to add products:

1. One at a time, with the full product form.
2. Several at once, in quick rows.
3. From a photo, with AI suggesting the details for you to check.
4. By importing a CSV file with SKUs, prices, low-stock levels and opening stock.

### Adding products with AI

Upload a photo and AI suggests the name, category, brand, description and any colours or sizes it can see. You
check the suggestions, add your price and quantity, and save. AI can also write or improve a product name or
description. AI never sets prices or stock; you always do.

### Variants: sizes and colours

Sizes, colours and other options are added as variants of one product. Each variant has its own SKU, barcode,
price and stock. Products can be organised by category and brand, with photos.

### Stock per branch

Products are shared across the business, and quantities are kept per branch. Every change to stock has a history
behind it: sales, returns, purchases received, transfers and adjustments, with who made each change.

### Correcting stock after a count

Record an adjustment with a reason such as stock count, damaged, expired or lost. The change is kept in the item's
history with who made it. Stock is never silently overwritten.

### Low-stock alerts

Set a low-stock level for each product. Items at or below that level are flagged on the dashboard, and StockỌja can
notify you in the app, by email or by push notification.

### Moving stock between branches

Send stock from one branch to another with a transfer. The sending branch records what left; the receiving branch
accepts what actually arrived, including part of a transfer. Both branches stay correct and the transfer stays on
record.

## Selling at the counter (POS)

### What the POS does

The point of sale is built for busy hours:

- Find items by barcode scan or by search. An exact barcode or SKU match goes straight into the cart.
- Discounts on a line or on the whole order.
- Coupons (on plans that include promotions).
- Hold a sale and come back to it.
- Choose or add a customer.
- Receipts for every sale, numbered by branch (for example IKE-000123).

### Payment methods at the POS

- Cash, with the change worked out for you.
- Card, on your own POS terminal.
- Bank transfer.
- A mix of these on a single sale (split payment).

There is no platform fee on POS sales.

### Barcode scanners and receipt printers

A barcode scanner that types into the search box works with the POS. Receipts are formatted for 80 mm thermal
printers, and every sale can be found later in sales history by receipt number or customer.

### Returns and voids

Returns are recorded against the original sale and put the stock back. Part of a sale can be returned. A voided
sale stays on record with the reason and who voided it, rather than disappearing.

### Selling when the internet goes off

The POS keeps selling without internet. Each sale is saved on the device with a receipt, stock goes down on the
screen, and everything syncs to StockỌja automatically, exactly once, when the connection returns. Open the POS
online once on each device first, so it has your products and prices.

Also works offline: recording expenses, adjusting stock, and returning items from sales made on that device.

Needs a connection: coupons, held sales, voids, editing expenses and adding new customers.

## The online store

### How the online store works

Every plan includes an online store at yourname.stockoja.com. It is not a separate website: it sells from the same
products, prices and stock you manage in the shop. Change a price once and it changes everywhere. Sell the last unit
at the counter and the store shows it as sold out. Online orders appear in your dashboard next to your counter
sales.

### Store design

Pick a ready-made theme, then add your logo, colours, banner and headline. Preview before you publish, and keep
editing afterwards. Shoppers choose a branch and see what is available there, including messages such as
"only 2 left".

### Pickup orders

A customer orders online and collects from your branch. They pay online through Paystack, or pay when they collect
(cash or card). The stock is held for them. You confirm the order, prepare it and mark it ready; the customer is
emailed at each step. When they arrive, you check their pickup code and hand the order over.

Order steps: Waiting (order placed, stock held, you are notified) → Confirmed → Ready for pickup → Picked up.

### Delivery orders

If you deliver, add each area you deliver to and what you charge, for example Lekki ₦2,500 or Ikeja ₦2,000, and
choose the branch deliveries are sent from. At checkout the customer picks pickup or delivery, chooses their area
and types their address. The delivery fee is added to the total and the whole fee is yours. Delivery orders are
paid online through Paystack.

Order steps: Waiting → Confirmed → Out for delivery → Delivered. The customer is emailed at each step.

You or your own rider make the delivery. StockỌja does not provide riders or couriers.

If you would rather not set fees, keep checkout as pickup only, or choose "I'll arrange delivery with customers
myself" and show a note such as "Call or WhatsApp us after ordering to arrange delivery".

### Stock is held automatically

An online order reserves its items, so two customers cannot buy the last one. Orders that are not completed in
time release the stock again.

### Getting paid for online orders

Add your bank account in Payouts. Paystack splits each online payment at checkout: your share goes straight into
your bank account, and StockỌja's platform fee, set by your plan, is taken from that payment. The Payouts page shows
sales, minus the platform fee, minus Paystack's fees, equals what was paid to your bank.

Orders paid on pickup are paid to you in person and carry no platform fee.

### Refunds for cancelled online orders

If an order the customer already paid for online is cancelled, the customer is refunded automatically through
Paystack. If a refund does not go through the first time, StockỌja keeps retrying and tells you if it still needs
your attention, so a refund is never lost or sent twice.

### Sharing your store

When you publish for the first time, StockỌja gives you a shop poster with your store's QR code. Download it as an
image or an A4 PDF, print it for the counter or window, or share it to WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook.

When you share your store or a product link on WhatsApp, Facebook or X, the preview shows a picture made from your
store (your banner, logo and name) or the product's photo, name and description. There is nothing to set up.

### Turning WhatsApp chats into orders

A lot of buying starts in a chat: a photo on WhatsApp status, a question in a DM. A store link turns that
conversation into an order with the price, the item and the payment already sorted, instead of a thread of messages
and a screenshot of a transfer.

## Branches, staff and suppliers

### Running more than one branch

Each branch (store) has its own stock, staff, sales, orders and expenses, under one shared product catalogue.
Owners can view one branch or the whole business. Your plan sets how many stores you can add.

### Staff roles and permissions

Invite staff by email and give each person a role. Built-in roles cover owners, managers and cashiers, and you can
create your own roles. A role is either business-wide or limited to one store; store-level staff only see their own
branch. A cashier can be limited to the POS at one branch.

Permissions cover things like selling, discounts, returns, stock adjustments, transfers, expenses and reports.

### Activity log

Sensitive actions are written to an activity log with the person, the time and the details. Staff who leave are
deactivated, not deleted, so their history stays intact.

### Suppliers and purchase orders

Keep a list of suppliers and raise purchase orders against them for a branch. The unit cost is filled in from the
product. Mark the order as ordered, then receive what arrives: if 6 of 10 come in, receive 6, and the order shows
as partly received until the rest arrive. Received stock goes straight into inventory at the cost you paid.

### Customers

Keep a list of customers, attach them to sales, and see who buys, how often and how much.

## Sales, expenses and reports

### The dashboard

The dashboard opens with today's sales against yesterday, the last 7 days against the 7 days before, gross profit
for the week and the number of open online orders. Below that: a sales chart, best sellers, items running low and
anything that needs your attention, such as orders to confirm.

### Sales tracking

Every sale is recorded as it happens: the items, the price after discounts, how it was paid, who sold it and at
which branch. Counter and online sales appear together, and can be split by channel. Because StockỌja knows the
cost price of what you sold, it shows gross profit alongside sales.

### Expense tracking

Record running costs in a few seconds: amount, date, category, branch and a short description. Ready-made
categories include rent, salaries, utilities, transport, supplies, marketing and repairs, and you can add your own.
Mistakes are voided with a reason rather than deleted. Expenses can be recorded offline and sync later.

Stock bought to resell is not an expense: it becomes inventory through purchase orders, and its cost is counted
when the item sells. Gross profit minus running costs shows what the business actually made.

### Reports and CSV export (paid plans)

From the Scale plan, reports let you pick a period and a branch:

- Sales: totals over time, by payment method, by channel (counter or online) and by branch.
- Products: ranked by revenue, units sold or profit.
- Inventory: stock on hand and its value.
- Customers: who buys, how often and how much.
- Export: sales, products, inventory, customers and expenses as CSV, for example for your accountant.

### Promotions and coupons (Thrive and Empire)

Coupons and automatic promotions across branches are included on the Thrive and Empire plans.

### Notifications

StockỌja notifies you in the app, by email or by push notification about things like new online orders and low
stock.

## Plans and pricing

### Pricing overview

StockỌja has four plans, priced in Nigerian naira. Every plan includes the full system: products, variants and
categories; inventory and stock history; the POS and receipts; customers, suppliers and expenses; and an online
store. Plans differ in how far you can grow: stores, staff, products, reports and promotions. Paid plans are billed
monthly in naira through Paystack.

### Thrive plan

### Empire plan

- Price: custom, agreed with you. Talk to the team.

### The platform fee on online sales

The platform fee applies only to online store orders that customers pay online through Paystack, and it is
deducted automatically from that payment. The higher the plan, the lower the fee.

- No platform fee on POS sales.
- No platform fee on orders paid on pickup.
- On delivery orders the fee is worked out on the goods only, never on your delivery fee.
- Paystack's own processing fee is separate.

### Changing plans and billing

Change your plan from Plan & billing inside the app. Paid plans are billed in naira through Paystack. If a renewal
payment fails, there is a grace period to pay; after that the business moves to the free plan's limits and
features until it is paid. Selling and access to your data are never blocked, and nothing is deleted.

## Affiliate programme

### How the affiliate programme works

Anyone can apply to share StockỌja with shop owners and earn a share of the subscription payments of the businesses
they refer. Joining is free; applications are reviewed, and the link starts working once approved. Existing
StockỌja users can join with their account.

1. Share your link: WhatsApp groups, social media, your website, or in person with a QR code.
2. They sign up: the sign-up is linked to you if they opened your link in the last 30 days. If they used several
   links, the most recent wins.
3. They subscribe: StockỌja verifies the payment with Paystack.
4. You earn monthly: each verified payment earns commission.

### Affiliate commission rates

Commission is earned on the amount actually paid for the plan, by the business's paid month:

- Months 1 to 3: 20%
- Months 4 to 6: 10%
- Month 7 onwards, while they stay subscribed: 5%

For example, a business on Scale (₦5,000 a month) earns you ₦1,000 a month in months 1 to 3, ₦500 in months 4 to 6
and ₦250 after that. On Thrive (₦15,000 a month): ₦3,000, then ₦1,500, then ₦750.

### Affiliate rules

- Only verified payments count. Sign-ups and free use earn nothing until the business pays.
- New commission is pending for 30 days (the refund period), then approved.
- Payouts: on the 5th of each month, approved commission from ₦5,000 is paid in naira to your bank account; smaller
  balances carry over.
- Commission is on plan subscriptions only, not on fees from merchants' online sales.
- A refund or chargeback reverses the matching commission.
- Signing up your own business through your link earns nothing.
- Do not promise discounts or features that do not exist, bid on "StockỌja" search ads, pose as StockỌja, spam
  groups, or share people's details without their permission.

## What StockỌja does not do yet

StockỌja would rather be clear about this:

- Own domain: online stores live at yourname.stockoja.com. Connecting your own domain is not available yet.
- Couriers: you can offer delivery, but StockỌja does not provide riders or couriers. You or your own dispatch
  partner make the delivery.
- Card payments at the counter go through your own POS terminal. StockỌja records the payment; it is not a card
  terminal itself.
- Online payments go through Paystack. Other payment gateways are not supported.
- There is no app to download from an app store. StockỌja runs in the browser on any device.

## Common questions

### Do I need to install anything?

No. StockỌja runs in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

### Is StockỌja really free to start?

Yes. The Hustle plan is free with no card needed: one store, two staff accounts, the POS and an online store.

### Can I use StockỌja on my phone?

Yes. The whole system, including the POS and the online store setup, works on a phone.

### Can I move from Excel or a notebook?

Yes. Import your products from a CSV file (Excel can save as CSV) with SKUs, prices, low-stock levels and opening
stock, or add them quickly in rows or from photos.

### Is my data safe and private?

Each business's data is kept separate from every other business. Staff only see what their role allows, sensitive
changes are logged, and records are corrected or voided rather than deleted.

### Do I need a website developer for the online store?

No. You choose a theme, add your logo and colours, and publish. Products, prices and stock come from what you
already manage.

### Can I sell on WhatsApp with StockỌja?

Yes. Share your store link or a product link on WhatsApp; the preview shows your picture, logo and name, and the
customer orders and pays through the store. The shop poster's QR code can also be shared on WhatsApp status.
